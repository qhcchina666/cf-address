How to Register a US Apple ID: Complete Tutorial with Address & Phone

📅 August 2026 · 🏷️ Apple ID · US App Store · Registration Guide

Creating a US Apple ID is the gateway to accessing apps, music, movies, and services that are exclusive to the US App Store. Whether you need a specific app unavailable in your country or want early access to new features, this step-by-step guide will walk you through the entire registration process — including how to handle the US address and phone number requirements.

1. Step-by-Step Registration Guide

Step 1: Sign Out of Your Current Apple ID

Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad. Tap your name at the top, scroll to the bottom, and tap Sign Out. You may be asked to enter your current Apple ID password to disable Find My. This step is essential — you cannot create a new Apple ID while signed into an existing one.

Step 2: Attempt to Download a Free App

Open the App Store, search for any free US-only app (e.g., a banking app or streaming service available only in the US), and tap Get. When prompted, choose Create New Apple ID. This method ensures your Apple ID is linked to the US region from the start.

Step 3: Select United States as Your Region

On the account creation screen, select United States as your country or region. This is the critical step that determines which App Store you'll access. Once set, the region cannot be changed without canceling active subscriptions.

Step 4: Agree to Terms and Conditions

Review and agree to Apple's Terms and Conditions for the US region. You'll need to tap Agree twice to confirm.

Step 5: Fill in Your Personal Information

Enter your email address (use a new email not associated with any existing Apple ID), create a strong password, and fill in your name. Set up security questions for account recovery. Choose answers you'll remember — these are used to verify your identity.

Step 6: Enter Your US Address and Phone Number

This is where most people get stuck. Apple requires a valid US address and a US phone number. See the sections below for detailed guidance on filling these in correctly.

Step 7: Select "None" for Payment Method

When you reach the payment method screen, you should see a None option. This option only appears when you create your Apple ID by attempting to download a free app. If you don't see "None," go back to Step 2 and make sure you initiated the process by downloading a free app.

Step 8: Verify Your Email

Apple will send a verification code to the email address you provided. Enter the 6-digit code to complete the verification and activate your new US Apple ID.

💡 Important: The "None" payment option is only available when you create the account through the App Store by downloading a free app. If you create the Apple ID through Settings or the Apple website, you may be forced to add a payment method.

3. Address Filling Tips

Apple's address validation is relatively lenient, but following these tips will help you avoid errors:

  • Use a real-looking address format. Even if the address is generated, it must follow the standard US address format: House Number + Street Name + Street Type (e.g., "123 Main Street").
  • City, State, and ZIP Code must match. Apple does cross-validate these fields. If you enter a ZIP code for Portland, Oregon but select "California" as the state, the form will reject it. Use our address generator to produce a consistent set.
  • Use the standard two-letter state abbreviation. Write "DE" not "Delaware." Apple's form uses a dropdown for state selection, so this is usually handled automatically.
  • Avoid PO Box addresses. While Apple may accept them, a residential-looking street address passes validation more reliably.
  • Don't use well-known public addresses. Addresses like "1600 Pennsylvania Avenue" or "1 Infinite Loop" are flagged. Use a randomly generated address for a smoother experience.

4. Phone Number Handling

Apple requires a US phone number during registration. This is primarily for two-factor authentication (2FA), which Apple strongly encourages. Here's how to handle this requirement:

Option 1: Use a Real US Number (Recommended)

If you have access to a US phone number — through a friend, a virtual number service (like Google Voice, TextNow, or Skype Number), or a prepaid SIM — this is the safest option. You'll receive verification codes via SMS or phone call.

Option 2: Skip Phone Verification

During the initial registration, you may be able to skip the phone number step. However, Apple will eventually prompt you to add a trusted phone number for 2FA. Skipping it is a temporary workaround, not a permanent solution.

Option 3: Use a Generated Number for Testing

For development and testing purposes, you can use a generated US phone number. However, this number won't receive verification codes, so it's only useful for non-critical accounts. Our address generator includes US phone numbers in standard format (e.g., "(212) 555-0198").

💡 Important: Apple's two-factor authentication requires a working phone number for account recovery. If you lose access to your Apple ID and don't have a trusted phone number, recovering your account becomes extremely difficult. Always associate a real, accessible phone number with your account.

5. Payment Method: How to Select "None"

The "None" payment option is the key to creating a US Apple ID without a US credit card. Here's exactly how to make it appear:

  1. Start from the App Store — Do not create the Apple ID from Settings. Open the App Store app instead.
  2. Download a free app — Search for any free app (not a paid or freemium app) and tap "Get."
  3. Choose "Create New Apple ID" — When the sign-in prompt appears, select this option.
  4. Complete the registration — Fill in your email, password, security questions, and US address.
  5. On the payment screen, select "None" — It should appear as the last option in the payment method list.

If "None" does not appear, you may have started the process incorrectly. Sign out of any Apple ID, restart your device, and try again following the steps above exactly.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between my US Apple ID and my local Apple ID?

Yes, you can sign out of one Apple ID and sign into another. However, each time you switch, any downloaded music, apps, or subscriptions tied to the other account become temporarily unavailable. You'll need to sign back in to access them.

Will Apple detect that I'm using a generated address?

Apple's address validation checks for format compliance and city-state-ZIP consistency, not whether the address is a real residence. As long as the address follows USPS format rules, it will pass validation. However, do not use this account for illegal purposes.

Can I add a payment method later?

Yes. You can add a payment method at any time through Settings > [Your Name] > Payment & Shipping. US App Store gift cards are a popular option for adding credit without a US bank card.

What happens if I forget my security questions?

If you have two-factor authentication enabled with a trusted phone number, you can reset your security questions. Without 2FA or a trusted phone number, recovering access becomes very difficult. This is why linking a real phone number is strongly recommended.
